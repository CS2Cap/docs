CS2Cap's liquidity score estimates how readily an owner can sell an item near its current market value. It is an item-level score from `0` to `100`, where higher values mean stronger confirmed sales velocity, executable fee-adjusted bids, competitive fresh listings, steadier pricing, and broader trustworthy market coverage.

The score is exposed on market analytics responses such as [`GET /market/items/item_id`](/api-reference/market-analytics#get-/market/items/item_id) and market snapshots. Liquidity is currently scored on the 24-hour horizon.

## Score summary

The normal score is the sum of five components:

| Component | Possible score (range) |
| --- | --- |
| Competitive sell-side depth | `0` -- `15` |
| Execution quality | `0` -- `25` |
| Confirmed sales velocity | `0` -- `45` |
| Price stability | `0` -- `5` |
| Data confidence | `0` -- `10` |

The final score is rounded to the nearest integer and clipped to the `0-100` range.

```text
liquidity =
  competitive sell-side depth
  + execution quality
  + confirmed sales velocity
  + price stability
  + data confidence
```

## Input data

Liquidity is built from current market data and recent activity:

| Input | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh competitive listings | Listings from providers whose best ask is within 10% of the current best ask. |
| Executable buy orders | Fresh same-provider bid/ask pairs, adjusted for the provider's instant-sell or seller fee and weighted by bid depth. |
| Confirmed sales | Steam and persisted marketplace sale counts blended across 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day windows. |
| Recent price observations | Per-provider hourly coverage and coefficient of variation, calculated within each provider/currency series. |
| Data confidence | Independent listing coverage, executable-book coverage, sales-data availability, and anomaly status. |

Prices are normalized to USD before ratio calculations. Bids and asks must come from the same provider; a cheap ask on one marketplace and a high bid on another cannot manufacture a perfect spread. Market documents older than six hours are ignored.

### Sell-side depth

Sell-side depth measures how much supply exists for the item right now.

- Range: `0-15`
- Curve: logarithmic
- Saturation point: `500` active listings

The logarithmic curve means that the distance in score between an item with 1 vs 20 listings will be greater than an item with 400 vs 500 listings.

Only providers with a best ask within 10% of the current best ask contribute. The result is clipped to `15`; distant or stale inventory adds no liquidity credit.

### Execution quality

Execution quality measures how much value an owner can realize by selling into a current buy order. Each bid is matched only with that provider's ask and reduced by the applicable instant-sell or seller fee.

- Range: `0-25`
- Fee-adjusted bid is 50% or less of the matched ask: `0` ratio points
- Fee-adjusted bid is 95% or more of the matched ask: full ratio points
- Bid depth contributes up to `5` of the `25` points
- Multiple providers are combined using logarithmic bid-depth weights

A tighter gap means buyers are already close to sellers, so the item is easier to exit without a large discount.

### Confirmed sales velocity

Confirmed sales velocity measures observed sales rather than decreases in listing quantity. Steam counters and persisted marketplace sale records are blended across three windows, so an expensive item is not treated as inactive solely because one day was quiet.

- Range: `0-45`
- Curve: logarithmic over the blended daily rate
- Saturation point: `100` confirmed sales per day

The 24-hour rate receives 50% of the blend, the 7-day daily average receives 30%, and the 30-day daily average receives 20%. Listing depletion, delistings, pagination changes, and inventory resets do not contribute.

### Price stability

Price stability rewards items that have been observed consistently and have not had extreme hourly price swings.

- Range: `0-5`
- Observation density max: `2.5` points
- Price stability max: `2.5` points
- Window: last 24h hourly close prices

Half of this component comes from observation density: an item seen in more hourly periods gets more credit than an item seen only once or twice.

The other half comes from price steadiness. Coefficient of variation is computed within each provider/currency series before the results are combined, avoiding false volatility from mixing currencies or marketplace price levels.

Price stability is supporting evidence only. It cannot compensate for missing confirmed sales.

### Data confidence

Data confidence rewards broad, trustworthy coverage without double-counting sales or listings.

| Signal | Max points | How it works |
| :---: | :---: | --- |
| Fresh listing-provider coverage | 4 | Saturates at 12 competitive providers |
| Same-provider execution coverage | 3 | Saturates at 3 providers with a usable bid and ask |
| Confirmed-sales data availability | 3 | Requires a fresh Steam snapshot or a successful persisted-sales query |

Same-provider bid-above-ask anomalies reduce confidence and are excluded from execution quality. Liquidity v2 has no price-based, rarity-based, Doppler, or high-tier boost. The legacy `high_tier_override` and `doppler_bonus` response flags remain for compatibility and are always `false` for v2 scores.

## API fields

Analytics responses ([`GET /market/items/*`](/api-reference/market-analytics#get-/market/items) endpoints) include the total score and component breakdown:

```json
{
  "liquidity": 61,
  "listing_score": 15,
  "gap_score": 22,
  "volume_score": 9,
  "stability_score": 5,
  "external_score": 10,
  "liquidity_last_updated": "2026-03-21T06:48:43.193895Z"
}
```

Use the component fields to understand why an item scored the way it did. For example, deep listings and strong bids cannot produce a very-liquid score when confirmed sales velocity is weak.

## Caching and freshness

Liquidity v2 uses its own Redis score and ranking namespace, preventing legacy and v2 results from mixing. Scores update as market data and confirmed-sales history change; stale cached scores can be served briefly while a recomputation is queued.

## How to interpret scores

| Score | Interpretation |
| :---: | --- |
| `80-100` | **Very liquid**. Strong confirmed velocity, executable bids, competitive depth, and broad coverage. |
| `60-79` | **Liquid**. Tradable under normal conditions, but one or two components may be weaker. |
| `30-59` | **Moderate or situational liquidity**. Check spread, depth, and recent volume before relying on fast execution. |
| `0-29` | **Low liquidity**. The item may be thin, stale, volatile, or poorly confirmed by external signals. |

Liquidity is a tradability signal, not a price forecast. A high score means the item is easier to buy or sell with less friction. It does not mean the item is underpriced or expected to rise.
